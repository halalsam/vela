02 Color

iOS system backgrounds and labels, a monochrome brand — black in light, white in dark — and three semantic states as the only hues. Every pairing below is checked against WCAG AA.

Paper · accent (dark)

Monochrome rules

The brand is ink on paper. Colour appears only where it carries meaning: semantic states, country flags and destination photography — so they read instantly.
